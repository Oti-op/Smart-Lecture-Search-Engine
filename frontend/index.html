How It Works

01

Upload your lecture recording

Drop any audio or video file — MP3, MP4, WAV, M4A, or WebM. No account or setup required.

02

AI transcribes and indexes the content

Whisper converts speech to text. The transcript is split into chunks and embedded into a vector index for semantic search.

03

Search by topic, get timestamped results

Type a question or topic in plain English. Get relevant segments ranked by relevance — with exact timestamps to jump to.
